### CHEM-000163: Sacrificial-reagent-free molecular photofixation of nitrogen
- **Subfield:** Nitrogen fixation and ammonia chemistry
- **Open:** Couple molecular nitrogen reduction to water oxidation so light, nitrogen and water are the only net inputs and ammonia and oxygen the net products.
- **Status:** Molecular fixation has advanced since the early 2000s. Yamazaki et al. achieved visible-light ammonia formation with water in 2025, while tertiary phosphines supplied sacrificial reducing equivalents.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** It would establish a materially closed artificial photosynthetic ammonia cycle.
- **Key works:** Y. Yamazaki, Y. Endo and Y. Nishibayashi (2025), *Catalytic ammonia formation from dinitrogen, water, and visible light energy*, [DOI:10.1038/s41467-025-59727-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-59727-w).
- **Related:** Physics: long-lived charge separation. Systems biology: artificial metabolic energy coupling.
